# Claim-Copy Audit — marketing and UI copy vs. what the product enforces

**Status:** audit only. No copy was edited, renamed, or deleted; no disclaimer was added to any new surface. Recommendations below are decisions for the product owner.
**Date:** 2026-09-01 · **Branch:** `agent/z-claim-copy-audit`
**Scope swept:** `README.md`, every file under `docs/` (excluding `docs/legal-panel/`), every UI string under `web/app/` and `web/components/`, and the certificate template. `service/scripts/certificate.html` no longer exists — the per-job custody certificate and the matter summary report are now generated inline by `_render_job_certificate_html` / `_build_certificate_html` and the `disclaimer` literals in `service/app/main.py` (lines ~239 and ~515), so those generated surfaces were audited at their current location. The upstream-utility surfaces (`docs/UPSTREAM_UTILITY_LEGACY.md`, `docs/synthid-text-benchmark.md`) were swept and are included; the research/commercial-surface manifests were swept and contain no legal-sufficiency claims (their hits are triage labels like `safe-to-keep`, which are file-disposition categories, not product claims — noted, not rowed).

**Terms swept (case-insensitive):** defensible, safe-sharing, immutable, court-proof, verified, compliant, SynthID detection, Google-compatible — plus near-synonyms found in context: certified/certification, guaranteed/guarantee, admissible, machine-verifiable, verifiable, "independently timestamped", unforgeable, unimpeachable, "safe to share", "write-once", "lossless", trust/trustworthy, "not externally anchored", "Chain verified".

## The yardstick

### Standing disclaimer, quoted exactly

`service/app/main.py:239-247` (matter summary report):

> "This report summarizes CounselClear's own recorded state for this matter -- document and job counts, open attention items, and audit chain integrity -- as of the generation timestamp below. It is **not** a legal certification, attestation, or a claim that any document is fully “clean” beyond what the per-job manifest and the hash-chained audit trail themselves record. Consult the individual job manifests for exactly what was stripped, flagged, or kept in each derivative."

The per-job certificate carries its own equivalent (`service/app/main.py:514-521`):

> "This certificate records CounselClear's own recorded state for this single job — what ran, what the applied policy did, what was verified, and what is explicitly disclosed as a limitation below — as of the generation timestamp. It is <strong>not</strong> a claim that this document is “clean,” “safe,” or free of risk beyond what is stated here, and it is <strong>not</strong> a legal opinion. Any limitation listed below is part of the certificate, not a defect in it."

A KEEP recommendation below means: the copy is at least as narrow as these disclaimers — it describes recorded state, not legal sufficiency.

### What the code actually enforces today

- **Verifier word discipline** — `tools/counselclear_verify_release_packet.py:105-109` (docstring): the tool "will never print 'unforgeable', 'independently timestamped', 'court-proof', or 'unimpeachable', and never the bare word 'VALID'". The enforcement lives in `tests/test_release_packet_verifier.py:517` (`test_forbidden_claim_words_never_appear_as_affirmative_claims`) and `tests/test_rfc3161_anchor_verification.py:995` (`test_report_rendering_avoids_forbidden_claims`); the exact banned set is the *affirmative* phrases `is unforgeable`, `this is unforgeable`, `is independently timestamped`, `this is independently timestamped`, `is court-proof`, `this is court-proof`, `is unimpeachable`, `this is unimpeachable`, plus the bare `this packet is verified` / `packet is verified`. Negations ("NOT EXTERNALLY ANCHORED") are the sanctioned pattern.
- **Verdict vocabulary** — the verifier reports `INTERNALLY CONSISTENT`, never `VALID`; signature outcomes are `VERIFIED` / `NOT VERIFIED (no --public-key given)` / `UNSIGNED (packet predates signatures)`; audit-chain seq numbers are "declared here, not verified" (line 364-365). With a TSA anchor: `Externally anchored: yes (rfc3161-tsa) -- TimeStampToken verified`; without: `CLAIMED ... anchor NOT verified`. The tool's own gloss (line 328): an operator signature "does not make the packet unforgeable or externally timestamped."
- **Standing proposals** — `docs/release-packet-verification-and-anchoring-proposal.md` §7 and `docs/release-control-evidence-thesis.md` §6 forbid "unforgeable", "independently timestamped"/"independently verifiable", "court-proof", "unimpeachable" as affirmative claims. `docs/rfc3161-anchor-implementation-proposal.md` §8: after TSA anchoring, "independently timestamped" becomes honestly available, but "unforgeable", "court-proof", and "unimpeachable" "remain forbidden regardless."
- **Claims doctrine** — `docs/counselclear-strategy.md` §5: never say "clean," "safe," "removed," "verified" unless bound to deterministic behavior + manifest/audit entry + regression test. `docs/COUNSELCLEAR_DESIGN.md:93` lists "Guaranteed removal of statistical or pixel-domain watermarks" as a v1 non-goal.
- **Preset names are out of scope** (per the audit brief): `evidence_preservation`, `production`, `external_sharing`, `privacy_only` are product presets, not certifications. No row judges the names; rows judge only surrounding copy.

## Findings

Recommendations: **KEEP** = at least as narrow as the yardstick disclaimer; **QUALIFY** = reads as broader than what's enforced and should be reworded or context-gated; **REMOVE** = affirmative legal-sufficiency claim with no honest reading.

## Summary

- **No affirmative legal-sufficiency claim was found in any customer-visible or generated surface.** The forbidden-claims discipline (`§7` list, strategy §5, certificate/summary disclaimers) is consistently applied in UI strings and generated artifacts; the doc corpus overwhelmingly uses the negation pattern (naming forbidden words in order to deny them).
- **The two structural exposure points are the word "Defensible"/"Safe Sharing" in design-doc headings and ledes** (B2, B4, B12/C12, B8) — internal today, but these lines are shaped like copy and are the most likely to be excerpted outward.
